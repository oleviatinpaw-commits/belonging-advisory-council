What We Do

Four task forces, one mission.

Read the full mission →
01

International Student Task Force

Understands the international student experience and works toward full community integration by collecting data on challenges, proposing targeted resources, and increasing participation visibility.

02

Student Engagement & Resources Task Force

Ensures every ethnicity and culture — particularly underrepresented groups — is recognized and celebrated through initiatives that highlight diverse individuals and foster inclusion.

03

Alumni Engagement Task Force

Maximizes the visibility of diverse alumni as mentors and role models by assessing alumni experiences and featuring their stories across campus.

04

Career Recruitment Task Force

Bridges the gap between underrepresented minority students and competitive opportunities by identifying barriers and building preparation pipelines.

Our Commitment

A council built by students, for students.

Council members work to create a welcoming environment, represent student perspectives at every level of the school, promote mutual respect, and celebrate the full diversity of the Smith student body.

Every voice matters in shaping the undergraduate journey — from first-year orientation to graduation and beyond.
