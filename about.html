About the Council

Our mission and how we carry it out.

Four task forces translate the council's mission into concrete work across the Smith undergraduate community.

Mission Statement

“The mission of the Belonging Advisory Council is to represent the collective views of the student body, empower students and provide comprehensive support in their undergraduate journey, foster a welcoming and belonging community, and unite Smith so that we may thrive together, rather than struggle separately.”

Primary Responsibilities

What council members do

Members work to create a welcoming environment for every student, represent student perspectives to school leadership, promote mutual respect across the community, and celebrate the full diversity of the Smith student body.

That work is organized into four standing task forces, each with a distinct focus and set of initiatives.

Our Structure

The four task forces

01 · International Student

International Student Task Force

Aims to understand the international student experience and ensure full community integration. The task force collects data on the challenges international students face, proposes targeted resources, and works to increase visibility and participation across campus life.

02 · Engagement & Resources

Student Engagement & Resources Task Force

Ensures that all ethnicities and cultures — particularly underrepresented groups — are recognized and celebrated. This task force runs initiatives that highlight diverse individuals and foster a stronger sense of inclusion at Smith.

03 · Alumni Engagement

Alumni Engagement Task Force

Maximizes the visibility of diverse alumni as mentors and role models for current students. The task force investigates alumni impact, assesses alumni experiences, and features diverse alumni stories through campus displays and events.

04 · Career Recruitment

Career Recruitment Task Force

Bridges the gap between underrepresented minority students and competitive career opportunities. This task force identifies barriers to access, communicates fellowship information, and builds preparation pipelines for high-demand roles.
