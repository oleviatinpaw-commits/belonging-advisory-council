Our Structure

The four task forces

01 · Post-Undergrad Education

Post-Undergrad Education Task Force

Helps undergraduates become aware of post-graduation pathways early — not just master's and PhD programs, but also financial courses, certifications, and other credentials that strengthen career readiness. The task force builds programming that makes these options visible well before senior year.

02 · International Student Support

International Student Support Task Force

Works to help international students access more resources for jobs and careers, particularly in light of current political challenges. The task force identifies gaps in career support, advocates for targeted programming, and works to ensure international students are fully integrated into Smith's professional ecosystem.

03 · Transfer Student

Transfer Student Task Force

Addresses the structural disadvantage transfer students face when prestigious clubs and organizations reserve applications for freshmen and sophomores. Also works to increase connectivity for transfer students, who often experience college as one-dimensional — come to class, do the work, leave — by building community and belonging from day one.

04 · Resources & Financial Resources

Resources & Financial Resources Task Force

Addresses the disconnect between Smith and students regarding helpful resources that improve the student experience. Focuses especially on students from underserved communities, who are often expected to obtain resources that are hard to access or don't exist. The task force works to close that information gap and advocate for real support.
