How Projects Happen

From student feedback to finished initiative.

Every project starts with something students tell the council — in surveys, at info sessions, or by email. A task force scopes the idea, proposes it to the full council, and carries it through to launch. Completed projects are documented here so the next council can build on them.

Have an idea for a project?

You don't need to be a council member to propose one. Send a short description of the problem you've noticed and we'll route it to the right task force.
