Join the Council

Help shape what belonging means at Smith.

The council recruits new members each semester. Here's exactly how the process works.

The Process

Four steps to joining

Review the mission

Read through the council's mission and four task forces so you can speak to why you want to join and which area fits you best.

Submit your application

Complete the official application form. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis each semester.

Attend an information session

The council hosts an All-Council Information Session each semester to walk applicants through current initiatives and answer questions. Dates are announced by email — reach out to smithbac@umd.edu for the next one.

Get matched to a task force

Accepted members are matched to one of the four task forces based on their interests and the council's current needs.

Ready to apply?

The application takes about 10 minutes. You'll be asked about your background, why you want to join, and which task force interests you most.

Other ways to stay connected

Attend an event

Task force initiatives run throughout the year — from cultural programming to alumni panels. Email us to be added to the council's event list.

Share feedback

You don't need to be a member to have a voice. Send the council your ideas or concerns at any time at smithbac@umd.edu.
